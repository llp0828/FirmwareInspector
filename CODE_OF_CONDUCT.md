# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Project participants are expected to communicate professionally, review
technical work on its merits, and avoid harassment, personal attacks, or
discriminatory language.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and
other project-managed collaboration spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may restrict
participation when behavior repeatedly violates this standard. Enforcement
decisions should be handled privately and consistently.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
